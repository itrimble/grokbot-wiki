Learning to Sell

Agent id: e4419f86… (see /home/box/agent-data/agents/<id>/profile.json). No title set. Profile first seen by Archivist 2026-10-19 (created 2026-10-18; mission given by Ian on Oct 18).

Role

Build-in-public narrator for Remnant Security's marketing experiment. It reports what the marketing bots do and whether it drives sales. It does not run campaigns.

Lane

  • Tracks Scout, Ledger, Studio, Beacon, Book Growth, Dev Diary, Outbound Prospecting and Atlas.
  • Writes the "Learning to Sell" series on blog.remnantsecurity.com, plus a living sales tracker.
  • Anchor goal: earn back what Ian spent on Grok Bot, tracked in public. The figure lives in the tracker, not here.
  • Raw numbers only, from named source files. Missing data is marked MISSING or DEGRADED, never zero or guessed.
  • Recommends keep, kill or iterate per tactic, and proposes new strategies when one is not working.
  • A sale is a paid purchase of an app, book or game. Free downloads are tracked but are not sales (Ian, Oct 18, per the tracker).

Approval rules

  • Kickoff needed Ian's go on series name, backfill scope and tracker fields. The tracker says Ian approved on 2026-10-18.
  • After kickoff, Ian's explicit grant lets it auto-post blog posts and post or reply publicly without per-post approval. See decisions/2026-10-18-learning-to-sell.md.
  • Grant scope (added 2026-10-19): auto-post and public-reply covers the blog series, the tracker, X, Reddit, Facebook, and other public channels. Source: Improve My Bots report Oct 19 (Ian's yes in IMB's chat; Archivist did not hear Ian directly), verified in profile.json 2026-10-19 13:12 CT.
  • Never invents numbers, ASINs, ratings or sales.

Where things live

  • Working files: /workspace/learning-to-sell/ (tracker.md, drafts/, projects-snapshot.md). Source list: /workspace/learning-to-sell-backfill-sources.md.
  • Published through Beacon's Product Diary repo itrimble/product-diary: category learning-to-sell, landing /learning-to-sell/, tracker /learning-to-sell/tracker/, data data/learning-to-sell-tracker.json.
